Unitsmillimetres, kilograms, degrees
Datumx from the outer face of the left posts, y from the front face, z up from the floor
Piece marksA, B, C… one letter per different piece. "x4" = cut four the same. Mirror images share a mark.
Cut marksFrom one square end, mark each edge at the two numbers, join the marks around the section, cut on the lines. Angles are for reference.
Edgesnamed by where they face on the finished build (top-front, bottom-right…)
Jointsmitre = same section, on the bisector · butt onto X = square onto the face of X · flush with X = cut level with X's outer face · then weld
Long pointthe overall length of the piece, tip to tip
Dimensionsjig drawings and joint tables give centre lines (℄), where the members' centres cross; the general arrangement gives outside sizes
