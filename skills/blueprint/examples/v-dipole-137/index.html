137 MHz V-dipole for weather satellites

Two quarter-wave rods at 120° in a horizontal plane, fed by an SMA jack in a 3D-printed hub on a PVC mast: the 9A4QV receive antenna for NOAA APT and Meteor LRPT near 137 MHz.

Fabricate · 1 h ·

Check A rod slides into each arm bore by hand without play you can feel; the mast pipe pushes into the socket to the end; all four inserts square and flush within ±0.5 mm.

A bore that came out tight is drilled out, never forced: forcing a rod in splits the arm along its layers.

02Wire the SMA jack

Fabricate · 0.5 h ·

Check Multimeter: centre pin to jack body open circuit (no short); centre pin to the east tail and jack body to the west tail both under 1 Ω.

Heat the solder cup briefly: the jack's plastic insulator melts if you hold the iron on for long.

03Elements: cut, tap and fit

Fabricate · 1.5 h ·

Check Both rods the same length within ±1 mm; end faces 12 ±1 mm apart; tip to tip 937 ±5 mm; each rod tight when you twist it.

The element length is measured from the rod's inner end face. Do not cut them shorter "to be safe": tuning in step 6 trims them, about 3.9 mm per MHz.

05Coax and coil choke

Assemble · 0.5 h ·

Check 5 turns, tight against each other and against the mast, starting within 50 mm of the hub; nothing slides when you tug the cable.

Parts

  • Coax, RG58, SMA male to SMA male x1 3.0 metre assembly (or the plug your receiver needs at the far end)
  • Cable ties, UV-stable black x10 200 long

06Tune with a NanoVNA

Tune and install · 1 h ·

Check SWR minimum within ±1 MHz of 137.5 MHz and SWR under 2 from 137.1 to 137.9 MHz; both elements still the same length within ±1 mm.

Trim both elements equally and a little at a time: a millimetre is easy to take off and impossible to put back.

07Seal, mount and point it

Tune and install · 1.5 h ·

Check Opening within 10° of north or south; each element level within 3° (a spirit level bubble inside the lines); the mast does not move when you shake it at the hub.

Mount it lower rather than in a place where the mast cannot be clamped at both marks: the wind check assumes two tight clamps on a post that does not move.

Parts

  • Screw, M3 × 8, pan head, stainless x2 lid into the heat-set inserts
  • Mast clamp (U-bolt with saddle) x2 M6 U-bolt for a 40 pipe, with a saddle for your post or wall bracket
