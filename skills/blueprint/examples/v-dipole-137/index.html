5Order

Start the hub print, then wire the jack and cut the arms while it runs; clamp the wires, clip them to the arms, fit the mast, tune by trimming the tips, then seal and mount.

  2. Wire the SMA jack 0.5 h
  3. Arms 0.5 h
  4. Element wires 1 h
  5. Clips and tip caps 0.5 h

  1. Coax and coil choke 0.5 h

Tune and install

  1. Tune with a NanoVNA 1 h
  2. Seal, mount and point it 1.5 h

7

Coax and coil choke

0.5 h

5 turns, tight, starting 50 mm or less under the hub. Nothing slides.

Parts

  • Coax, RG58, SMA male to SMA male x1 3.0 metre assembly (or the plug your receiver needs at the far end)
  • Cable ties, UV-stable black x10 200 long
8

Tune with a NanoVNA

1 h

SWR minimum ±1 MHz of 137.5 MHz. Under 2 from 137.1 to 137.9 MHz. Both tips equal ±1 mm.

Trim a little at a time: a millimetre cannot be put back. Bend nothing at the tips.

9

Seal, mount and point it

1.5 h

Open within 10° of north or south. Arms level within 3°. No movement when shaken at the hub.

Both clamps tight on a post that does not move: the wind check counts on them.

Parts

  • Screw, M3 × 8, pan head, stainless x2 lid into the heat-set inserts
  • Mast clamp (U-bolt with saddle) x2 M6 U-bolt for a 42 mm pipe, with a saddle for your post or wall bracket
